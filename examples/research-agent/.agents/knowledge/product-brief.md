# Product brief

The research agent supports developers who need a trustworthy first pass over
an unfamiliar topic. It is optimised for questions that can be answered from
public sources, not for private or paywalled material.

Success looks like: a short answer, a ranked evidence list, and an explicit
list of open questions. It is not a chatbot persona exercise; tone stays
neutral and factual.
